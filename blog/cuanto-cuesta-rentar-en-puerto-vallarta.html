Every few months someone posts in a Facebook expat group asking what rent costs in Puerto Vallarta, and every few months they get 40 conflicting answers ranging from "$400 a month in a great apartment" to "you can't touch anything decent under $2,000." Both are technically true, which is to say neither is useful.

We run furnished rentals in Puerto Vallarta, Guadalajara, and Bahía de Banderas. We're not a platform, a blog, or an aggregator — we're local property owners. What follows are real numbers, drawn from what we charge and what we see in our neighborhoods right now, in August 2026.

At today's exchange rate (~17–18 MXN/USD), a furnished studio for $8,000 MXN/month works out to roughly $450–$470 USD/month. A two-bedroom in a residential neighborhood runs around $900–$1,100 USD/month. Zona Romántica is a different world — people pay $1,500–$2,500 USD for the same square footage purely for the walkability and the address.

Prices by neighborhood — what you actually get

Colonia Primavera

This is where our studios are, and we'll be transparent about why we're here: it's genuinely one of the best value-for-location neighborhoods in the city. Colonia Primavera is residential — no tourist bars, no bus noise, mostly families and professionals. It's about 15 minutes by car from the malecón and walking distance from supermarkets, pharmacies, and good local restaurants.

Fraccionamiento Los Sauces

A quieter residential area further north, popular with families and longer-stay expats. Two-bedroom furnished apartments run $1,000–$1,200 MXN/night on Airbnb. Our Departamento Los Sauces goes for $1,066 MXN/night — it sleeps 5, has a full kitchen, A/C in every room, TV, and laundry. For monthly stays, it's worth asking directly.

Versalles

One of the most in-demand neighborhoods right now. Centrally located, walkable to restaurants and the hospital corridor. Studios start around $10,000–$14,000 MXN/month for furnished units. Unfurnished is cheaper but then you need to source everything yourself.

Zona Romántica (Emiliano Zapata)

The most expensive and most tourist-forward neighborhood. If you're there for a week, the energy is unbeatable. If you're planning to live and work remotely for three months, you'll pay a significant premium for location you may not fully need. Budget $20,000–$40,000 MXN/month for a decent furnished apartment.

Fluvial Vallarta / Marina

Modern buildings, good security, closer to the airport. Popular with professional expats and families. Prices are mid-range to high: $15,000–$25,000 MXN/month for a furnished two-bedroom.

Local tip

If you're choosing between Col. Primavera and Zona Romántica, ask yourself how often you'll actually walk to the malecón. Most long-term residents quickly settle into a neighborhood routine — a good coffee shop, a market, a gym — that has nothing to do with the tourist strip. The quiet neighborhoods are a better daily life. Save Centro for weekends.

2BR apartment, Los Sauces 30 nights ~$32,000 MXN $16,000–$20,000 MXN $12,000–$16,000 MXN

The platform adds a 14–20% service fee on top of whatever the owner charges. For a month-long stay, that adds up to real money — often more than an entire extra week of rent. This is why booking directly with the owner makes financial sense the moment you're staying longer than two weeks.

That doesn't mean Airbnb is always wrong. For 3–7 nights, the platform offers security, reviews, and a dispute process that's worth the fee. The logic shifts when you're planning a longer stay and you've already done your research on the property.

What "furnished" actually means here

In Mexico, "amueblado" can mean anything from a mattress and a fan to a fully equipped apartment ready to host a small company offsite. You need to ask. When we say furnished, we mean:

  • Bed with linens, pillows, and towels
  • Mini-fridge or full refrigerator
  • Microwave, coffee maker, basic utensils
  • A/C (mini-split, not a ceiling fan)
  • High-speed WiFi (we test ours — minimum 50 Mbps download)
  • Private bathroom with hot water
  • Wardrobe / closet space
  • A table or desk to work from

What's usually not included in a studio: washer/dryer (often shared or laundry nearby), and in some cases the electricity bill is billed separately. Ask upfront. The ones who hide these details tend to have other surprises too.

What to watch out for

The "direct booking" bait-and-switch

Some landlords list on Airbnb at a certain price, then once you're in contact, push you to book "direct" at a price that's only marginally lower but with no platform protection. Legit direct booking means a signed rental agreement, receipts, and a clear cancellation policy. If someone only offers WhatsApp photos and a cash transfer, walk away.

Electricity bills in studios

CFE (Mexico's national electricity company) charges residential rates for the first tier and significantly higher for above-threshold usage. Some landlords split this honestly; others use it as a hidden cost. Ask whether electricity is included in the monthly rate, and if not, what the average bill runs. An A/C-heavy summer studio can easily add $1,500–$3,000 MXN/month to your costs.

Lease minimums

Monthly rentals in residential neighborhoods typically require a minimum of 3 months. Some owners want 6. This protects them from revolving-door tenants and gives you a lower nightly effective rate. If you're planning to stay 1–2 months only, Airbnb is often your only realistic option — accept the fee, or negotiate directly with a specific property you trust.

"The people who get the best deals in Puerto Vallarta are the ones who arrive, spend a week in an Airbnb to get oriented, and then find their long-term place in person. You can negotiate things by email, but you close deals in person."

Frequently asked questions

Can I negotiate rent in Puerto Vallarta?

Yes, especially for stays of 2 months or more. Most owners would rather have a reliable tenant for three months than chase Airbnb guests every week. Come prepared with a clear timeline, references if you have them, and be upfront about your situation. Honesty closes deals faster than haggling.

Is Puerto Vallarta more expensive than it used to be?

Yes. The pandemic remote-work wave, the depreciation of the peso in 2022–2023, and sustained Canadian/American demand have pushed prices up significantly since 2020. A studio that was $6,000 MXN/month in 2020 is now $9,000–$12,000. That said, it's still well below comparable coastal cities in California, Florida, or BC.

What's the cheapest way to rent in Puerto Vallarta long-term?

Arrive in person, get oriented for a week on Airbnb, then walk or drive the residential neighborhoods (Primavera, Versalles, 5 de Diciembre, Fluvial) looking for "Se Renta" signs. Talk to owners directly. This is how locals find places. You'll find unfurnished units cheaper than anything online — but you'll need to furnish them yourself, which adds cost and time.

Is it safe to rent directly without using Airbnb?

With a legitimate owner, yes. Get a signed rental agreement that specifies the monthly rate, what's included, the deposit amount, and the cancellation terms. Pay by bank transfer (SPEI/CoDi), not cash, so you have a paper trail. If an owner refuses to sign anything, that tells you everything you need to know.

La plataforma agrega entre 14 y 20% de comisión de servicio encima de lo que cobra el propietario. En una estancia de un mes, eso suma dinero real — muchas veces más que una semana entera de renta adicional. Por eso reservar directo con el propietario tiene sentido financiero desde el momento en que tu estancia supera las dos semanas.

Eso no significa que Airbnb siempre sea mala opción. Para 3 a 7 noches, la plataforma ofrece seguridad, reseñas y un proceso de disputa que vale la comisión. La lógica cambia cuando planeas una estancia más larga y ya investigaste la propiedad.

Qué significa "amueblado" aquí

En México, "amueblado" puede significar desde un colchón y un ventilador hasta un departamento completamente equipado listo para una oficina. Necesitas preguntar. Cuando nosotros decimos amueblado, significa:

  • Cama con sábanas, almohadas y toallas
  • Mini refrigerador o refrigerador completo
  • Microondas, cafetera, utensilios básicos
  • A/C (mini-split, no ventilador de techo)
  • WiFi de alta velocidad (el nuestro lo medimos — mínimo 50 Mbps de descarga)
  • Baño privado con agua caliente
  • Ropero / espacio de almacenamiento
  • Mesa o escritorio para trabajar

Lo que generalmente no incluye un estudio: lavadora/secadora (frecuentemente compartida o lavandería cercana), y en algunos casos la factura de luz se cobra por separado. Pregunta de antemano. Los que esconden estos detalles suelen tener más sorpresas.

Qué tener en cuenta

El engaño de la "reserva directa"

Algunos propietarios anuncian en Airbnb a cierto precio y luego, una vez en contacto, te presionan a reservar "directo" a un precio apenas más bajo pero sin protección de plataforma. La reserva directa legítima implica un contrato firmado, recibos y una política de cancelación clara. Si alguien solo ofrece fotos de WhatsApp y transferencia en efectivo, retírate.

Facturas de luz en estudios

CFE (la empresa eléctrica nacional de México) cobra tarifas residenciales para el primer bloque y significativamente más por consumo superior al umbral. Algunos propietarios dividen esto honestamente; otros lo usan como costo oculto. Pregunta si la luz está incluida en la renta mensual, y si no, cuál es la factura promedio. Un estudio con A/C en verano puede agregar fácilmente $1,500–$3,000 MXN/mes a tus costos.

Estancias mínimas

Las rentas mensuales en colonias residenciales generalmente requieren un mínimo de 3 meses. Algunos propietarios piden 6. Esto los protege de inquilinos rotativos y te da una tarifa efectiva por noche más baja. Si solo planeas quedarte 1 o 2 meses, Airbnb suele ser tu única opción realista — acepta la comisión, o negocia directo con una propiedad específica en la que confíes.

"Los que consiguen los mejores precios en Puerto Vallarta son los que llegan, pasan una semana en un Airbnb para orientarse, y luego encuentran su lugar de larga estancia en persona. Puedes negociar cosas por correo, pero los tratos se cierran en persona."

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Se puede negociar la renta en Puerto Vallarta?

Sí, especialmente para estancias de 2 meses o más. La mayoría de los propietarios prefieren un inquilino confiable por tres meses que estar persiguiendo huéspedes de Airbnb cada semana. Llega preparado con un calendario claro, referencias si las tienes, y sé directo sobre tu situación. La honestidad cierra tratos más rápido que el regateo.

¿Es Puerto Vallarta más caro que antes?

Sí. La ola de trabajo remoto de la pandemia, la depreciación del peso en 2022–2023, y la demanda sostenida de canadienses y americanos han subido los precios significativamente desde 2020. Un estudio que costaba $6,000 MXN/mes en 2020 ahora está en $9,000–$12,000. Dicho esto, sigue muy por debajo de ciudades costeras comparables en California, Florida o BC.

¿Cuál es la manera más barata de rentar en Puerto Vallarta largo plazo?

Llega en persona, oriéntate una semana en un Airbnb, luego camina o maneja las colonias residenciales (Primavera, Versalles, 5 de Diciembre, Fluvial) buscando letreros de "Se Renta". Habla con los propietarios directamente. Así es como los locales encuentran vivienda. Encontrarás unidades sin amueblar más baratas que cualquier cosa en internet — pero necesitarás equiparlas tú mismo, lo que agrega costo y tiempo.

¿Es seguro rentar directo sin Airbnb?

Con un propietario legítimo, sí. Obtén un contrato de arrendamiento firmado que especifique la renta mensual, qué incluye, el monto del depósito y las condiciones de cancelación. Paga por transferencia bancaria (SPEI/CoDi), no en efectivo, para tener comprobante. Si un propietario se niega a firmar cualquier cosa, eso te dice todo lo que necesitas saber.
